My thoughts...

I think the scoring was reasonably fair enough in this round. The reindeer skull is clearly the most unusual, and the grape scissors aren’t far behind. If you saw either item in someone’s house, you’d question it. Romesh’s snowglobe is not really something you’d question as being unusual if you saw. I’ve no idea what Roisin’s rationale for her picture was, and Josh’s was rubbish, even if the signatures had mysteriously disappeared. Even with the football being signed, he’s getting 1 point.

Task 1

Eat as much watermelon as possible. You have 1 minute. Your time starts when you open the door to the lab.

My thoughts...

Alex’s face while watching Tim is an absolute joy. This one is hard to re-score, as it’s a simple case of how much watermelon is left at the end of the time. It’s a pretty factual outcome. The task mentions about eating watermelon within 1 minute and Greg was clear that “if it’s inside you after 1 minute, it counts”. Interestingly, Alex immediately contradicts that by saying he weighed Romesh’s vomit (which happened after 1 minute) as “watermelon remaining”. So, Romesh should have won on merit anyway. The whole “penalising Tim for the wink” is largely irrelevant.

My thoughts...

I debated being harsh to Roisin, disqualify her and give her 0 points for not riding the same horse as everyone else. Greg did penalise her and gave her last place and 1 point. But I think that’s very harsh. The task wasn’t to ride that specific horse. The task is to paint a horse whilst riding a horse, and technically, Roisin did do that. A mechanical horse is still a horse. So, I think that’s fine. I much preferred her picture to Romesh’s as well, so I’m switching their positions. Everyone else deserves the position they got.

Task 3

Completely empty this bathtub. Fastest wins. You must not remove the plug, tip or damage the bath.

ContestantResultGreg's ScoreMy ScoreChange

My thoughts...

This was another “factual” task where it’s tough to score it any other way. It’s simply fastest wins. Josh did ask during the task “does it need to be completely dry?” and presumably Alex says yes, and he runs off to get a towel. Roisin just doesn’t bother at all. Tim and Romesh get towels brought to them which speeds them up, but Josh could have asked for that. I do think Tim was a little unlucky the plug came out. Greg drops him to last place but I think rules are rules, and it’s a disqualification. Tim gets 0.

Lets see how that continues through the episodes……
