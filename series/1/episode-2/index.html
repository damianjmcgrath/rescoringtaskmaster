My thoughts...

For probably the first time, Greg’s scoring is all over the place. The London Marathon is obviously the most impressive, although slightly boring, but that’s fair enough. After that, Greg goes a bit rogue. Josh is very harshly treated - I think winning Pointless is impressive, especially as Josh got a pointless answer by naming Julian Barnes as the author of The Sense of an Ending. His complaints after are entirely justified! Frank’s leather hat is very cool, and I’m easily impressed by that but he didn’t make it or even think of the idea. Romesh just caught a hat, and Roisin just bought some champagne.

My thoughts...

Little fun fact – this was filmed in the Eden Shopping Centre in High Wycombe. Right, let’s get a few things out of the way first. Firstly, I don’t care about Tim using charity – no task rules were broken (even though it was entirely unnecessary, the guy was totally willing to do a high five without Tim’s story)! Secondly – Romesh’s sign was one of the worst made things I’ve ever seen! Thirdly, anyone who didn’t high-five a single 55 year old is getting disqualified, so that’s Frank and Romesh out! After that, it’s just done on time. Josh, Tim, then Roisin.

My thoughts...

Another task, another controversy. What constitutes a breach? We’ll come to that in a minute. To start with, Frank’s performance here deserves a mention. It’s literally the least amount of effort possible – he just guesses 5 pies. The fact he guessed Steak for 3 of them, and missed the actual steak one is wonderful. And “Minty Tipex” is one of my favourite quotes across all series! Now….a breach? As always, the task dictates the rules. It says “you cannot breach”. Emphasis on the “you”. If you break that rule, it’s disqualification. So, Josh and Romesh are out. Tim and Roisin (although Roisin was very bad at it!) got Alex to breach it, that’s fine. Now, here’s another controversy – in the show, Roisin got 4 points, for a reason I still can’t figure out. She didn’t appear to get any correct, and Romesh got 3 correct with no sign of being disqualified and only received 3 points. Absolutely no idea what Greg’s system was here, but luckily, we’re here to correct it! Roisin at least put in more effort than Frank, so she can have second and Frank gets third.

Romesh steals the show with Tree Wizard. Everything about it was brilliant. Tim’s was good fun as well, although he slightly let himself down with MILF. Frank in particular produced probably the worst reverse-video that’s ever been made but at least Greg only gave him 1. For Josh to get 4 points for his is outrageous!

Final thoughts...

Well, this is the first episode where there’s significant re-scoring, and a new episode winner. Roisin gets her victory taken away, mainly because I didn’t rate her massive champagne and thirsty wolf reverse film as highly as Greg did. Most people received reduced points, which is probably a sign I’m going to be a lot more strict than Greg. The other thing to note about this episode is how awful Frank was at most of the tasks! From episode 1 winner to episode 2 last place is quite a turn around for him!
