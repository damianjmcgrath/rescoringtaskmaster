Re-scoring Taskmaster

Same Tasks. Better Scores.

Taskmaster's scoring is part judgement, part chaos. This site goes back through every episode, re-scores the tasks, and asks one simple question: what would happen if the points were awarded differently?

What is this site?

Every episode is reviewed task by task. Greg's scores are compared with my own, questionable decisions are reconsidered, and the alternative scores are carried through the whole series. The aim isn't to remove the chaos from Taskmaster — it's to see whether different scoring would actually change anything.

What is Taskmaster?

Taskmaster is a comedy game show in which five contestants complete a series of unusual, inventive and often ridiculous tasks. The Taskmaster, Greg Davies, awards points for their efforts, assisted by the show's creator Alex Horne. Contestants accumulate points across each episode and throughout the series, with the highest-scoring contestant eventually crowned series champion.
